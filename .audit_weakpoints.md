## 7. Lead-to-meeting conversion gap
- Meetings are created ONLY when the owner replies "haan" to a digest email: sba_autopilot.py:709-723 `_process_replies` parses owner commands, computes a slot via `meeting_slot(lead, OWNER_TZ, hour)` and calls `meetings.create_meeting()`; lead status -> "meeting".
- A lead saying "yes" never books: sba_autopilot.py:744-756 classifies the reply, then emails the owner "New interested lead!" and sets status to "owner_confirm" (755); the owner must then reply to trigger booking. Comment at 747-748 states it explicitly: "The workspace owner (client or agency) is the one who must book the meeting, not the lead who just said 'yes'."
- sba_meeting.py:46-126 `create_meeting` (Meet link -> calendar event -> store -> confirm email) is the only booking path, invoked only from the owner-confirm flow / admin API; no lead-initiated entry point.
- NO self-serve booking link anywhere: sba_email_draft.py has zero calendar/booking/Calendly/Meet references; outbound CTA is just "Would you be open to a quick 15-minute call this week?" (sba_email_draft.py:27, prompt line 48). sba_email_templates.py likewise has no booking link, and the owner notification (sba_pipeline.py:213-224) instructs the owner to reply "Haan" by email.
- Net: the funnel is single-threaded through owner email replies (lead "yes" -> owner "haan [time]"); the lead can never self-serve book, so conversion stalls whenever the owner doesn't reply promptly.
